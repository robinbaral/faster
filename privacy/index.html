Privacy Policy

Faster: Intermittent Fasting · Last updated November 1, 2026

I'm Robin Baral, and I make Faster, a fasting timer, on my own.

I don't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Your data stays on your device

Your fasts, goals, settings and theme choice are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. If your device is backed up to iCloud or your computer, this data is included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in the Settings app.

Purchases

The theme purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no personal or payment information from it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
