Privacy Policy

Faster: Intermittent Fasting · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version: Faster doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your fasts stay on your iPhone.

I'm Robin, and I make Faster on my own. This policy explains exactly what that means.

What data Faster collects

None. Faster doesn't collect, transmit or store any personal information or usage data on any server, mine or anyone else's. It makes no network requests of its own, and it contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.

Faster doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it doesn't use your data for advertising.

What stays on your device

To work, Faster saves the following on your iPhone only, in storage shared between the app and its widgets:

  • your current fast, your fasting goal and your past fasts (start, end and goal),
  • your settings, such as reminders and your chosen theme.

This data never leaves your device through Faster. I can't see it and have no way to access it.

Notifications

Reminders, such as when your eating window is about to close or when you reach your goal, are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Faster asks for your permission before sending any, and you can turn them off in the app or in the Settings app at any time.

Services provided by Apple

Some things happen through Apple rather than through Faster. They're covered by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Purchases. The optional theme purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I don't receive your name, payment details or any other personal information from it.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Faster's data is included in that backup like any other app's.
  • App Store statistics. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated information such as download numbers, usage counts and crash reports. Apple doesn't share anything that identifies you, and you can turn this off at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Sharing with third parties

Faster doesn't share data with anyone, because it doesn't collect any. Nothing is sold, rented or passed on.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept on your iPhone for as long as Faster is installed. You can delete individual fasts in Settings › History. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device permanently. Since I hold no data about you, there's nothing for me to delete or hand over on request, but you're always welcome to ask.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because Faster doesn't collect personal data, I don't hold any information about you to which these rights could apply. If you have a question about your data, contact me using the details below.

Children

Faster isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security purposes as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how Faster handles data changes, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Faster? Write to me at fasterfastingapp@proton.me.
